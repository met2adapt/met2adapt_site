The training programme

High-level research and supervision

A holistic training experience with state-of-the-art laboratory facilities, hands-on technical training and expert scientific supervision across all host institutions.

Personal Career Development Plan

Each Doctoral Candidate co-designs a Personal Career Development Plan (PCDP), calibrated to their scientific strengths and long-term professional aspirations.

What it includes
  • Enrolment in the doctoral school of the host institution.
  • Formal training activities targeting transferable skills.
  • Structured progress monitoring and strategic mentoring sessions, to stay aligned with project objectives and address research challenges early.

Gender balance and inclusion

Gender balance and inclusiveness are actively promoted throughout the Met2Adapt programme.

How we put it into practice
  • Expert-led workshops on inclusive leadership, cultural competency and recognising implicit bias, as part of the core training modules.
  • An Equal Opportunities Officer monitors diversity metrics, from the applicant pool to DC retention and satisfaction, and feeds the results back into our strategies.
  • Flexible working arrangements: parental leave, childcare responsibilities and part-time options are respected and supported without penalising career progression or publication output.

Thematic Training Weeks

Network-wide events organised by the beneficiaries, where all Met2Adapt scholars and associated partners work directly with the Doctoral Candidates through lectures, personal tutorials and workshop-style sessions.
