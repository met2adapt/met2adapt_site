What is a meta-material? Stop the wave

Below is a chain of masses connected by springs. The gold driver on the left shakes it at the frequency you choose, and a wave travels along the chain to the right.

Now switch on the meta-material: the masses in the middle section each get a small oscillator (a gold hexagon). Without it, the wave passes through untouched at every frequency. With it, frequencies close to the oscillators' own natural frequency are stopped: the resonators move against the chain and the wave dies out inside the section. This frequency range is called a band gap.

Try it: switch the meta-material on, then slide the frequency into the shaded band in the chart.
