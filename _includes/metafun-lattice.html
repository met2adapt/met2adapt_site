Spin a unit cell

A meta-material is built by repeating a small unit cell over and over, like bricks in a wall. Engineers design the shape of that cell to get the behaviour they need: very light yet stiff, energy-absorbing, or able to block vibrations.

  • Honeycomb: the shape in our logo. Light and stiff, used in aircraft panels and sandwich structures.
  • Cubic: the simplest lattice, with struts only along the edges.
  • BCC (body-centred cubic): adds diagonal struts to the centre, a common choice for 3D-printed lattices.
  • Octet truss: struts arranged as tetrahedra and octahedra, among the stiffest lattices for its weight.
