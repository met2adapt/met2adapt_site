<h2>Our training philosophy</h2>
<p>The Met2Adapt training programme combines cutting-edge research projects with a comprehensive curriculum of scientific, technical and transferable skills. To this end, Met2Adapt brings together a team of world-leading academic and industrial experts and puts in place an innovation-driven training programme. Our overarching aim is to deliver highly qualified individuals with a multidisciplinary skillset across the spectrum of meta-materials and structural engineering, renewable energy production, computer programming, and the modelling of life-cycle impacts in the energy sector. They will be able to drive forward the long-overdue integration of advanced materials in renewable energy production. Key to our training methodology is our firm commitment to an active, student-centred ‘training by research’ environment, which puts the Doctoral Candidates in charge of their training.</p>
